About SkillBridge

SkillBridge is a skill-sharing platform designed to connect people who want to learn new skills and share the skills they already know. It creates a simple environment where members can discover others with similar learning interests.

In today's world, many people have valuable skills but may not have the opportunity to teach them to others. At the same time, many learners want practical knowledge without spending a lot of money. SkillBridge helps bring these two groups together.

The idea behind SkillBridge is simple: “Share Skills, Grow Together.” Instead of learning only through traditional methods, members learn directly from people with real experience.

Members create profiles with their name, a skill they can teach, a skill they want to learn, a short bio, and an optional photo — helping others understand their interests at a glance.

Searching is simple: someone who wants to learn Python can search for people who know Python, while someone who knows Web Design can offer that skill to others.

Under the hood, SkillBridge runs on a Flask backend with a real SQL database: passwords are hashed (never stored in plain text), and every profile lives securely on the server.
